`tema`, `t3`, `trix`, `tsi` and `massi` document the `adjust` and `sma` options they forward to their EMAs, with ema's defaults: `adjust` is `False` (`tema` and `t3` said `True`) and `sma` (seed each EMA with an SMA) is `True`. In `tsi` they reach the EMAs of the TSI line, not the signal moving average.
