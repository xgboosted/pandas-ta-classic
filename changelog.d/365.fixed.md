**BREAKING — `fosc`, `dpo(centered=False)`, `pvt`, `nvi` and `pvi` now equal TA-Lib's functions of the same name**: `fosc` uses the previous bar's forecast (it equalled `cfo`), the uncentered `dpo` is the price `length // 2 + 1` bars ago less the SMA, `pvt` uses the fractional change instead of percent (results are 100 times smaller), and `nvi`/`pvi` multiply instead of add (their scale changes: on SPY_D `nvi` ends at 185592 instead of 1538). The default centered `dpo` does not change.
