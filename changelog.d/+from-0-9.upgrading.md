**Check these when you upgrade from 0.9.0**: the package needs `pandas>=3.1.0` and `numpy>=2.4.6`, and `custom.load_indicator_module()` is removed. Results change for `rsi` and `qqe` on a series that has not moved yet (50, not 0.0), for `kama` (bar `length - 1` is NaN) and `psar` (equal to TA-Lib's `SAR`), for `squeeze`, `squeeze_pro` and `qstick` with a `mamode`, `ma` or `use_tr` other than the default, and for `fillna`, `fill_method` or `offset` on some option paths. With `talib=True`, a parameter TA-Lib cannot express now runs natively instead of being ignored, and `bbands`, `apo` and `ppo` take the same `mamode` on both paths, so `"kama"` and `"mama"` raise `ValueError`.
