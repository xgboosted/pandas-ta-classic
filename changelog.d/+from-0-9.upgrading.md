**Check these when you upgrade from 0.9.0**: the package needs `pandas>=3.1.0` and `numpy>=2.4.6`; `custom.load_indicator_module()` is removed, and a custom indicator may not reuse a package name. An unknown keyword raises `TypeError`; `strategy()` passes each indicator only the keywords it accepts. A non-default parameter that changes the values is part of the column name. Price-relative indicators (`roc`, `ppo`, ...) read NaN and warn once at prices at or below zero. Results change for `rsi` and `qqe` on a flat start, `kama`, `psar`, `fisher` and `msw`; `fosc`, the uncentered `dpo`, `pvt`, `nvi` and `pvi` equal TA-Lib (`nvi`, `pvi` change scale); seven indicators (`dema`, `kc`, `qstick`, ...) honour their moving-average options; `fillna` and `offset` change on some option paths. With `talib=True`, a parameter TA-Lib cannot express runs natively, and `bbands`, `apo` and `ppo` take the same `mamode` on both paths. `stc`'s `ma1`, `ma2` and `osc` must have close's index.
