**Check these when you upgrade from 0.9.0**: the package needs `pandas>=3.1.0` and `numpy>=2.4.6`; `custom.load_indicator_module()` is removed, and a custom indicator may not reuse a name the package has. Indicators relative to the price level (`roc`, `ppo`, `mcgd`, ...) read NaN with one `UserWarning` at a price at or below zero. Results change for `rsi` and `qqe` on a flat start, `kama`, `psar`, `fisher` and `msw`; `fosc`, the uncentered `dpo`, `pvt`, `nvi` and `pvi` now equal TA-Lib (`nvi` and `pvi` change scale); `squeeze`, `squeeze_pro`, `qstick`, `dema`, `kc`, `apo` and `ppo` honour their moving-average options; `fillna` and `offset` change on some option paths. With `talib=True`, a parameter TA-Lib cannot express runs natively, and `bbands`, `apo` and `ppo` take the same `mamode` on both paths. `stc`'s `ma1`, `ma2` and `osc` must have close's index.
