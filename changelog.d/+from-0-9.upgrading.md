**Check these when you upgrade from 0.9.0**: it needs `pandas>=3.1.0` and `numpy>=2.4.6`; `custom.load_indicator_module()` is removed, and a custom indicator may not reuse a package name. An unknown keyword raises `TypeError`, and `strategy()` passes each indicator only those it accepts; `fast` above `slow` raises, and flags such as `asmode` take only True, False or None. A non-default parameter that changes values is in the column name. Price-relative indicators read NaN, with one warning, at prices at or below zero. Results change for `rsi` and `qqe` on a flat start, `kama`, `psar`, `fisher` and `msw`; `fosc`, the uncentered `dpo`, `pvt`, `nvi` and `pvi` equal TA-Lib (`nvi`, `pvi` change scale); seven indicators honour their moving-average options; `fillna` and `offset` change on some option paths. With `talib=True`, a parameter TA-Lib cannot express runs natively, and `mamode` takes the same values on both paths. `stc`'s `ma1`, `ma2` and `osc` must have close's index.
