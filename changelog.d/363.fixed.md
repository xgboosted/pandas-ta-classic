**BREAKING — `fisher` and `msw` no longer depend on the price unit**: both compared against a fixed 0.001 in price units, so prices quoted at a small scale (sub-dollar stocks, crypto pairs in BTC) gave other results. `fisher` now treats only a window whose range is a float residue of its price as flat (position 0.0, where the old floor gave -0.5), and `msw` uses the limit of its phase where the real part is zero, which also changes 2 bars of SPY_D at its own price level.
