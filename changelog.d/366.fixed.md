**BREAKING — a price at or below zero reads NaN, with one warning**: an indicator relative to the price level (`roc`, `ppo`, `natr`, `log_return`, `mcgd`, ... 31 in all) reads NaN on such bars and raises one `UserWarning` naming itself and the number of bars, instead of a flipped sign or a value that explodes near zero; the values come back after those bars, as after missing ones. `bbands`' `BBB`, `accbands`' bands, `cpr`'s width percentage and `drawdown`'s `DD_PCT`/`DD_LOG` do the same where their own level is at or below zero. `mcgd` no longer steps past the price.
