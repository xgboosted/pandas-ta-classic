**New indicator `higuchi_fd`**: the Higuchi fractal dimension of the close over a rolling window (`length=30`, `kmax=5`, column `HFD_30_5`), after Higuchi (1988). It reads about 1 on a trend, 1.5 on a random walk and 2 on noise, does not change with the price level or scale, and reads 1 on a flat window, a straight line.
