**BREAKING — `min_periods` outside 1 to `length` was accepted by `mad`, `median`, `quantile`, `stdev` and `variance`**: a value above `length` computed with `length` instead without a word, and `min_periods=0` left the last bar NaN (`quantile` raised `IndexError`). These calls now raise `ValueError` naming the indicator, as `kurtosis` and `skew` already did. In `sma`, `midpoint`, `midprice`, `willr`, `vortex`, `cmf`, `beta`, `correl` and `donchian` (`lower_min_periods`/`upper_min_periods`) a value above `length` already raised pandas' `min_periods 6 must be <= window 5`; the error now names the indicator and its bounds. Valid calls are unchanged.
