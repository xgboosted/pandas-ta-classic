**`ta.defaults(name)` and declared defaults**: the moving averages and the indicators that take `mamode` declare their defaults and constant bounds in the signature (`length: Annotated[int, Bound(gt=1)] = 10`), so `inspect.signature(ta.hma)` shows `length=10` and `ta.defaults("hma")` reports the default, the bounds or choices and the options read from `**kwargs`. `None` still selects the default; results and error messages are unchanged.
