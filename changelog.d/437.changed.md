**Every indicator declares its defaults**: the declaration that #436 gave the moving averages and the `mamode` indicators now covers the whole registry, so `inspect.signature()` and `ta.defaults()` show the default and bounds of every parameter that can be declared; what stays in the body depends on another parameter, gives `None` a meaning, takes a list, or is a free-form string. `None` still selects the default, and results are unchanged.
