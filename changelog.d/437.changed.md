**Every indicator declares its defaults**: the declaration that #436 gave the moving averages and the `mamode` indicators now covers the whole registry, so `inspect.signature()` and `ta.defaults()` show the default and bounds of 190 of the 224 indicators; the others take no parameter besides `offset` and `drift`, or only ones whose bound depends on another parameter. `None` still selects the default, and results are unchanged.
