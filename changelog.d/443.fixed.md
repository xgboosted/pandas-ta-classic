**BREAKING — `volatility`, `downside_deviation` and `sortino_ratio` annualise by the number of intervals between returns**: the periods per year counted the n returns over a span that holds n − 1 intervals, so every result came out sqrt(n / (n − 1)) too large (sqrt(2) for two returns, 5.4% for ten, 0.01% on 20 years of daily bars). Evenly spaced bars now count exactly one year / step per year, and `sharpe_ratio(use_cagr=True)` changes through `volatility`.
