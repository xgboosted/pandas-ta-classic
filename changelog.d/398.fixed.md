**BREAKING — `linreg` and the indicators built on it raise `ValueError` for `length=1`**: a line through one point read NaN on every bar with a `RuntimeWarning`, and `cti` and `linreg(r=True)` read 0.0 on every bar. `linreg`, `linregangle`, `linregintercept`, `linregslope`, `tsf`, `cfo`, `cti`, `fosc`, `inertia` and `po` now raise, naming themselves; `squeeze(lazybear=True)` needs `kc_length` above 1, and a one-bar window handed to linreg through `mamode="linreg"` or `qstick(ma="linreg")` raises too, also where only the smoothed column was empty (`stoch(d=1, mamode="linreg")` returned %K and an empty %D). Use a length of 2 or more.
