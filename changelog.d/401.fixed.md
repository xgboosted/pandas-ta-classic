**BREAKING — `cpr` checks its options whether or not their feature is on**: `virgin_lookforward` was checked only with `virgin_cpr=True`, and `width_narrow` and `width_wide` only with `width_analysis=True`, so a value such as `virgin_lookforward="yes"` or `width_narrow=-1` was accepted without a word when the feature was off. Each now raises `ValueError` naming `cpr()` and the option; remove the option or pass a valid value.
