**BREAKING — native `psar` now equals TA-Lib's `SAR` bar for bar**: bar 2's stop was bounded by bars 1 and 0 instead of bar 1 alone, so the opening bars of some series differed until the first reversal, and a low (long) or high (short) that only touched the stop did not reverse the trend, so a flat run held the stop at the price. Both now follow `SAR`; on an all-flat series the trend reverses on every bar.
