**A window too short for the chosen moving average names the parameter you passed**: with `mamode="hma"` or `mamode="linreg"`, a window of 1 raised the moving average's own error, naming a `length` the indicator does not have, for example `stoch(d=1, mamode="hma")` raised `stoch() length must be an integer > 1`. It now raises `stoch() d must be an integer > 1 with mamode='hma', got 1`, in `adx`, `adxr`, `amat`, `aobv`, `apo`, `hilo`, `kvo`, `ppo`, `qqe`, `squeeze`, `squeeze_pro`, `stoch`, `stochf`, `stochrsi` and `tsi`; valid calls are unchanged.
